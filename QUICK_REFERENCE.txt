│ 💡 KEY TECHNICAL INSIGHTS                                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Why Separate Normalization Works:
  ──────────────────────────────────
  • Airports are flat (horizontal >> vertical extent ~250:1)
  • Unified scale compressed Z-axis to [-0.008, 0.008]
  • Separate scales give each axis appropriate resolution
  • Both axes still use full [-1, 1] range in normalized space

  Why Denormalization Metadata Matters:
  ─────────────────────────────────────
  • Neural network outputs are in normalized space
  • Must convert back to original UTM coordinates for use
  • Dict structure enables clean mathematical inversion
  • Metadata stored with data for easy reconstruction
